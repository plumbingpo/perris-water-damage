Flood Prevention for Homeowners in Perris, CA

Home / Blog / Flood Prevention for Homeowners in Perris, CA

While you can't control the weather, you can take proactive steps to protect your Perris, CA property from flood damage. A prepared home takes far less of a hit when it floods, and the savings in restoration costs can reach the thousands. Here Perris Restoration shares the preparation steps that work best for homeowners in Perris.

Understand the Flood Risk You Face

Understanding your property's flood risk is the first step in preparation. Check FEMA flood maps to determine whether your property is in a designated flood zone. Even if you're not in a high-risk zone, consider that most flood claims come from outside designated flood zones. Look at how your land slopes, how it drains, and how close it sits to any waterway that might overflow in heavy rain.

Ways to Protect the Property

On the physical side, keep a sump pump with a backup battery installed and maintained, store valuables and key documents higher than ground level, grade the lot to fall away from the foundation, lift electrical panels and HVAC units above where floodwater could reach, seal any foundation cracks and add a waterproof coating, and clean gutters and downspouts while running the discharge out to 6 feet or farther from the foundation.

Getting Your Insurance in Order

Flood damage caused by rising water is not covered under a standard homeowner's policy. Consider purchasing flood insurance through the NFIP or a private carrier, and since there is typically a 30-day waiting period before coverage begins, don't wait for a forecast to act. Document your property and belongings with photos and video for insurance purposes. Store your policy paperwork where you can get to it, and learn your deductible and coverage limits.

Your Plan for a Flood Emergency

Planning has to happen before floodwater is on the way. Know your evacuation routes and have a go-bag ready.
