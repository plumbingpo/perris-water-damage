Preparing Your Home for Storms in Perris, CA

Home / Blog / Storm Damage Preparation Checklist in Perris, CA

Storms can cause devastating water damage to homes and businesses in Perris, CA. Getting ready ahead of the season, and again ahead of each storm, can significantly cut both the damage to your property and what restoration costs later. Use this Perris Restoration checklist to see what preparing a Perris home involves from start to finish.

Pre-Season Preparation

Ahead of the season, do the following: check the roof for flashing or shingles that are missing, damaged, or worn out. Give the downspouts and gutters a thorough cleaning and make sure they discharge properly. Look over every door, window, and exterior penetration, and seal around each one. Make sure the sump pump passes inspection and its backup power system works in a test. Go over your insurance policy, including what it says about wind and flood damage. For insurance records, photograph the property as it is now. Trim trees near your home to reduce wind-driven branch damage.

When a Storm Is on the Way

Once a storm is forecast, tie down loose outdoor items before they can turn into projectiles. If conditions call for it, board up the windows or close your storm shutters. Confirm that the sump pump runs and that its backup battery is charged. Put important documents and valuables in waterproof containers or carry them to an upper level. Know your evacuation route and have supplies ready. Save the Perris Restoration emergency line, (973) 730-8803, in your phone contacts.

After the Storm Ends

After the storm passes: Check for damage from a safe distance before entering. Document all damage with photos and video. Cover roof openings with tarps if safe to do so. Do not enter flooded areas until electrical hazards are assessed. Let your insurer know about the damage.
