Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Across Perris, Perris Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Why Water Extraction Is Not a DIY Job

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Professional extraction also addresses moisture that you cannot see or reach with consumer tools. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Add our number, (973) 730-8803, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Store a bucket and spare towels where you can grab them, along with the number for a restoration company

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Carpeting so wet it squelches when you step on it or has puddles on top
  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
